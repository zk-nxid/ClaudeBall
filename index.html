How to Play

Controls

  • Move the mouse to aim the cue.
  • Click the table to lock the aim (click again, right-click or Esc to unlock).
  • Drag the Power slider at the bottom, then release to shoot.
  • Arrow keys (or the on-screen ◀ ▶ on touch screens) fine-tune aim.
  • Touch: drag on the table to aim, lift your finger to lock, then drag the slider.
  • With ball in hand, click to place the cue ball behind the head string (left two diamonds).
  • M toggles sound.

Rules (simplified WPA)

  • Break: pocket a ball or drive 4 balls to a rail. An 8 on the break is re-spotted.
  • The first ball legally pocketed after the break picks solids (1-7) or stripes (9-15).
  • Keep shooting while you pocket your own balls.
  • Foul (scratch, wrong first ball, or no ball hit): opponent gets ball in hand behind the head string.
  • Clear your group, then pocket the 8-ball to win. Sinking it early or fouling on it loses.

Play Online

Online play needs an internet connection. Local and computer games stay fully offline.
